# Medical Examination Device (The "Microscope")

## Overview
As part of the **Astrobiology** curriculum (specifically Chapter 04: The Truth of Observation), students build the **Medical Examination Device** acting as a digital "Microscope." This hardware serves as the primary tool to look inward, observing the cellular, biometric, and functional genomic signals that define living organisms.

Developed in consultation with biomedical researcher Prof. Felix Mesak, this device shifts the student's perspective from the macroscopic (Telescope/LiDAR) to the microscopic.

## Prologue of Spacetime Role
Where LiDAR establishes the external environmental geometry, the Microscope maps the internal topology of an organism. It answers the fundamental Astrobiology question: How does observation behave when looking inward to study systems biology and functional expression?

### Connection to Prof. Felix Mesak's Focus
Prof. Mesak's work on extremophiles, gene expression, and biomedicine forms the theoretical backbone of the experiments conducted with this device, ensuring students are engaging with real-world biological concepts, from cellular diapause to genetic regulation.
