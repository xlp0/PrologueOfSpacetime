# Duwi's Workshop Setup Plan

## 1. Front Glass Museum & Display Area (Front Room)
- **Front Glass Desk**: A dedicated display space acting as a mini-museum.
- **Function**: This area is strictly for showcasing all the completed projects, prototypes, and products that Duwi has created over time to document his journey. (No retail products).
- **Layout**: Shares the main open space with the CNC & Soldering area.
- **Safety**: Fire extinguisher required.

## 2. Main Workshop Area (CNC & Soldering - Front Room)
- **Heavy Machinery**: 
    - CNC machine
    - Laser cutter
    - 3D Printers (UV Resin and Regular FDM/Filament)
- **Electronics & Assembly**:
    - Dedicated soldering stations
    - PCB fabrication and assembly equipment
    - Workbenches for hardware building
- **Layout**: Shares the main open space with the Museum area.
- **Safety**: Fire extinguisher required.

## 3. Back Office & Research Lab (Enclosed Room)
- **Enclosed Room**: A separate room separated by a door from the front Museum/Workshop area. It has its own air conditioning for temperature control.
- **Function**: Lab, research, and quiet work.
- **Equipment**:
    - Microscopes
    - Recording equipment (audio/video for documentation)
    - Computer / Networking workstations
- **Safety**: Fire extinguisher required.

## 4. Outdoor Testing Area

- **Function**: Dedicated outdoor zone specifically for hazardous or combustion-based experiments.
- **Usage**: Testing engines, working with fire, and high-risk mechanical operations.
- **Safety**: Fire extinguisher required (Heavy Duty / Class B/C recommended).

## 5. Warehouse / Storage
- **Environment**: Currently experiencing high humidity.
- **Status**: Some stored equipment has broken or degraded due to the damp conditions. Dehumidification or climate control solutions need to be implemented.
- **Safety**: Fire extinguisher required.
