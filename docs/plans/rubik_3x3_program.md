# Rubik's Mastery: 30-Day Speed Challenge

Welcome to the **Rubik's Mastery Program**. over the next 30 days, you will transition from a beginner to a speed-cubing expert. This document outlines the curriculum, weekly milestones, and English communication practice required to succeed.

## 2. Weekly Curriculum

### Week 1: Foundations & Initial Benchmarks
*Objective: Master the fundamental mechanics and pieces.*
- **Action Items**: 
    - Identify Cube Anatomy (Center, Edge, and Corner pieces).
    - Memorize Standard Notation (**R, L, U, D**).
    - Perfect **The White Cross** and **White Corners** placement.
- **Reference Video**: Start to 5:15
- **English Practice**: "Hello! These are the **centers**, which remain stationary. This is an **edge**, and this is a **corner**. I am currently constructing the **White Cross**."
- **Target Performance**: **3 Minutes** (Full Solve)

### Week 2: Intermediate Algorithms
*Objective: Complete the first two layers and establish the top structure.*
- **Action Items**:
    - Master the **Second Layer** edge insertion.
    - Execute the **Yellow Cross** formation.
    - Practice the "Righty" and "Lefty" movements until they are fluid.
- **Reference Video**: 5:15 to 9:20
- **English Practice**: "Observe this sequence. I am applying the **Righty Algorithm** (R U R' U') to secure this edge. The second layer is now complete."
- **Target Performance**: **1 Minute** (Full Solve)

### Week 3: Advanced Optimization
*Objective: Solve the final layer and implement speed-oriented techniques.*
- **Action Items**:
    - Align and orient **Yellow Edges**.
    - Resolve **Yellow Corner** positioning and orientation.
    - Adoption of **Finger Tricks** to replace full-hand movements.
- **Reference Video**: 9:20 to Finish
- **English Practice**: "I am currently **orienting** the final corners. To increase speed, I am using finger flicks rather than rotating my entire hand."
- **Target Performance**: **Sub-60 Seconds**

### Week 4: Mastery & Peer Mentorship
*Objective: Achieve consistency and demonstrate technical literacy.*
- **Action Items**:
    - **Consistency**: Maintain a sub-1 minute solve time across five consecutive attempts.
    - **Mentorship**: Explain the full resolution process to a peer using English terminology.
    - **Strategic Look-Ahead**: Predict subsequent moves during current executions.
- **English Practice**: "I have achieved mastery of the 3x3 cube. The process begins with the cross, followed by algorithmic layer resolution. Let's review the steps together."

## 4. Guidelines for Success
1. **Persistence**: The learning curve can be steep; technical errors are opportunities for refinement.
2. **Linguistic Immersion**: Always utilize standard English cubing terminology during explanations.
3. **Instructional Reinforcement**: Teaching the method to others is the most effective way to solidify your own understanding.
