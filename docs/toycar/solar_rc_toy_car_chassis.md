# DIY Solar RC Car Chassis Design Guide

Based on the specific dimensions of your components (59.34x59.34mm solar panel, 99.58mm axles, and a 20.41x37.59mm motor), the chassis of your toy car needs to be carefully proportioned.

---

## 1. Optimal Dimensions

To perfectly accommodate your parts, your chassis should target these dimensions:

*   **Width:** **65 mm to 75 mm**
    *   *Why?* Your axles are 99.58 mm long. A 70 mm wide chassis leaves about 14.79 mm of axle sticking out on both sides. This provides ample space to attach your wheels securely without them rubbing against the sides of the chassis during operation. Additionally, it perfectly comfortably fits the 59.34 mm width of the solar panel.
*   **Length:** **110 mm to 140 mm**
    *   *Why?* You need a flat 59.34 x 59.34 mm area on top specifically for the solar panel. Furthermore, you will need linear space to mount the motor (37.59 mm long), as well as extra room for an RC receiver board, gears, and potentially a backup battery.
*   **Ground Clearance:** **~15 mm to 20 mm**
    *   *Why?* The motor has a width/diameter of 20.41 mm. You need the chassis and gear system to have enough ground clearance so it does not scrape the ground. Ensure the wheels you select are large enough (e.g., 30mm to 40mm in diameter) to keep the chassis slung low but safe from surface bumps.

---

## 2. Recommended Materials

Instead of heavy cardboard or thick plastic (like some DIY kits), upgrade to one of these materials to dramatically improve speed and battery/solar efficiency:

### C. 3D Printed Frame (PLA / PETG) - *Best for Precision*
*   **Pros:** You can exact-fit the motor mounts and axle holes.
*   **Hack:** Design a "skeletonized" frame with cutouts to reduce weight, while leaving a solid 5x5cm flat plate on top specifically for your solar panel.
