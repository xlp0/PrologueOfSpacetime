# The Five “Dining Philosophers” (Five Pillars of Energy Communication)
## 1. God of Communication
**Core function:** Transmission of meaning.
This pillar represents the **ability to exchange ideas, emotions, and information**. Communication is the foundation of all coordination between individuals and societies.
**Key aspects**
* Language
* Symbols
* Dialogue
* Persuasion
* Cultural storytelling
**Energy communication meaning**
Information itself is treated as a form of **energy transfer between minds**.
**Examples in religions**
* **Hinduism:** Sacred mantras and Sanskrit scriptures
* **Islam:** Revelation through the Qur’an
* **Buddhism:** Teaching of Dharma
* **Christianity/Catholicism:** Gospel preaching and scripture
Communication becomes the **medium through which truth and wisdom propagate**.
---
# 2. God of Energy
**Core function:** Power and vitality that sustains existence.
This pillar represents **life force, movement, and the driving power of the universe**.
**Key aspects**
* Physical energy
* Spiritual vitality
* Motivation
* Transformation
**Energy communication meaning**
Energy is the **carrier that allows systems to function and evolve**.
**Examples**
* **Hinduism:** Prana (life force)
* **Islam:** Divine power of Allah sustaining creation
* **Buddhism:** Impermanence and dynamic existence
* **Christianity/Catholicism:** Holy Spirit as divine power
Energy represents **the force behind action and change**.
---
# 3. God of Knowledge
**Core function:** Understanding and wisdom.
This pillar symbolizes the **accumulation, organization, and transmission of knowledge**.
**Key aspects**
* Learning
* Wisdom
* Science
* Philosophy
* Enlightenment
**Energy communication meaning**
Knowledge converts **raw information into structured understanding**.
**Examples**
* **Hinduism:** Vedas and philosophical traditions
* **Islam:** The pursuit of knowledge encouraged in the Qur’an
* **Buddhism:** Enlightenment through insight
* **Christianity/Catholicism:** Theology and scholastic traditions
Knowledge becomes **the intellectual framework guiding civilization**.
---
# 4. God of Time
**Core function:** Structure and continuity.
Time governs **change, history, and causality**.
**Key aspects**
* Cycles
* History
* Destiny
* Growth
* Decay
**Energy communication meaning**
Time determines **when communication and energy manifest**.
**Examples**
* **Hinduism:** Cycles of creation (Yugas)
* **Islam:** Linear history and the Day of Judgment
* **Buddhism:** Cycle of rebirth (Samsara)
* **Christianity/Catholicism:** Salvation history from creation to redemption
Time provides **the temporal dimension of existence**.
---
# 5. God of Technology
**Core function:** Tools that amplify human capability.
Technology represents **the extension of human intelligence and power**.
**Key aspects**
* Innovation
* Tools
* Infrastructure
* Artificial systems
**Energy communication meaning**
Technology accelerates **the speed and reach of communication and knowledge**.
Examples today include:
* The internet
* Artificial intelligence
* Global communication networks
Technology transforms **local communication into global civilization networks**.
