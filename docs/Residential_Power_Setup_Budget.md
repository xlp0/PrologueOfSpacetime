### Battery Runtime Benchmarks
- **16 kWh battery** running 4 kW load (multiple ACs): ~3.5 hours without solar
- **5 kWh battery** running single computer: 20-25 hours
- **15-20 kWh battery** (recommended for this project): Can cover half-day usage including AC with 9.6 panel units
- **36 kWh battery** needed for 12 hours nighttime operation with standard load

### Installation Location Requirements
**Indoor Placement (Kitchen Area):**
- **Location:** Kitchen area (or back area near toilet)
- **Critical Requirements:** Protected from direct sunlight and rain
- **Co-location:** Battery and inverter installed together in same indoor space

## Pre-Configured System Packages

### Option 1: Full 6 kW Hybrid System
- **Components:** 10 panels (6 kW), 16 kWh battery, smart inverter, installation
- **Package Cost:** 140,000,000 IDR
- **ROI:** ~70 months (5.8 years) based on 2M/month savings
- **Example:** Reduces 5M monthly bill to ~3M

### Option 2: UPS System (Minimum Solar)
- **Components:** 4 panels (2.5 kW), battery, inverter, installation
- **Package Cost:** 80,000,000 IDR
- **Use Case:** Emergency backup only
- **Reliability:** Powers essential equipment (Wi-Fi, laptops, phone charging) during blackouts

### Option 3: Starter System (No Panels)
- **Components:** 6 kW inverter, 12 kWh battery
- **Package Cost:** 60,000,000 IDR
- **Use Case:** Add panels later
