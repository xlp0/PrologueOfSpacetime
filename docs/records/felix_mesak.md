# Prof. Felix Mesak

## Meeting Context
This document serves as background research for the meeting regarding the integration of the **Medical Examination Device** into the "Microscope" project spanning the Astrobiology curriculum of *Prologue of Spacetime*.

## Career Overview
**Dr. Felix Mesak** is an established biomedical researcher with a PhD, specializing across several deeply analytical domains of biology:
- Molecular Biology
- Systems Biology
- Functional Genomics
- Transcriptomics
- Cancer Research & Radiopharmaceuticals

### Key Affiliations
- **University of California, Irvine (UCI)**: Department of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology
- **BC Cancer Agency (Canada)**: Involved in molecular studies and human disease research

## Notable Research Focus
His academic literature highlights several key areas perfectly aligned with our Astrobiology framework (which focuses on cellular structure, resilience, and observing biological truth):

1. **Evolutionary Biology & Extremophiles**
   - At UC Irvine, he performed critical studies on the **mangrove rivulus fish** (*Kryptolebias marmoratus*).
   - This organism is fascinating because it can undergo **diapause**—the ability to literally pause its own biological development to survive extreme conditions. This touches heavily on themes of Astrobiology and finding life in extreme environments.

2. **Cancer Research & Optical Tracking**
   - He has significant published work on the **radioimaging of cancer-related molecules**.
   - This involves using radiotracers and advanced medical imaging to track how genes are expressed ("turned on or off") in things like colorectal cancer and tumor cells. 

3. **Functional Genomics**
   - His work bridges the gap between raw genetics (the code) and functional transcriptomics (what the code actually produces in the organism's systems).

## Implications for the Medical Examination Device
Because his expertise bridges advanced optical/medical imaging and deep cellular biology/genomics, he is the ideal consultant for the "Microscope" project.

The goal of the device is to bring biological truth gathering to the students. Prof. Mesak can provide exactly the correct "biomarkers" or optical sensor ideas that will be both accessible to build and scientifically profound to observe.
