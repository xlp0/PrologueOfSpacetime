# QA Resume Positioning — Translation, Not Invention

## Insight
For QA-targeted roles, the issue was not "bad resume" but **positioning**. Ilham's master resume read as "AI operations / data / RLHF / multilingual evaluator" — fine for AI companies, muddy for normal QA recruiters. The fix is **translation, not fabrication**: reframe what he already did into **quality operations / evaluation QA / process QA / performance QA** language. His actual work *was* QA-adjacent — calibration consistency, quality analytics, evaluation standards, bottleneck analysis, onboarding/SOP/training standardization, multilingual QA review, performance-trend reporting. Real QA work, just not in the old-school "test case / bug ticket / regression testing" sense.

**Title changes:** from `AI Operations & Quality Analytics Specialist` to **`Quality Assurance & Quality Analytics Specialist`** (or `Quality Assurance Analyst | AI Operations & Quality Analytics` to preserve the AI angle; or `Quality Analyst | Process Improvement & Performance Analytics` for pure quality/process roles). Don't lead with "AI Operations" if the target job is normal QA — that's burying the lede.

**Summary rewrite:** *"Quality Assurance and Quality Analytics professional with experience improving evaluation consistency, calibration standards, and process quality across multi-project operations. Increased calibration alignment from 70% to 90% across 26 Quality Analysts, supported training programs with 50+ contributors, and produced quality analytics to identify bottlenecks and improve performance. Skilled in quality assurance, root cause analysis, KPI reporting, process improvement, Python, SQL, Tableau, and Excel."* Opens with QA, keeps the measurable win, sounds legible to ATS and humans, still truthful.

**Skills reorder:** Quality Assurance & Operations (Quality Assurance, Quality Analytics, Calibration & Evaluation Standards, Root Cause Analysis, Process Improvement, Performance Reporting, Workflow Optimization, Training & Onboarding, SOP/Knowledge Base Documentation, KPI Tracking) → Data & Analytics (Python, SQL, Tableau, Excel, Statistical Analysis, Data Visualization, Data Validation) → AI/Specialized Evaluation (Model Evaluation, RLHF, Prompt Evaluation, Data Annotation, Multilingual Evaluation, Reward Modeling). Put the recruiter's language first; put the niche jargon after.

**Role title parentheticals:** `Calibrator / Language Representative (Quality Assurance & Multilingual Evaluation)`; `Quality Analyst`; `Senior AI Data Trainer (Quality Evaluation & Data Operations)`. The parenthetical does a lot of heavy lifting — truthful, not fabricated, but recruiter-friendly outside the AI bubble.
