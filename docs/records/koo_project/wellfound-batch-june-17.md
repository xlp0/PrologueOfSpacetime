# Wellfound Batch — June 17 Small AI Companies

## Insight
A 2026-06-17 batch of small AI company applications via Wellfound, all positioned around AI evaluation / quality operations as the bridge from Ilham's AI Trainer/QA/Calibrator past to an AI-ops lead role, rather than pretending to be a senior ML engineer.

**Drytis** ($4.28–17.15/hr, Delhi/NCR, contract) — "AI Computer Science Engineer (Prompt Engineering & Client Support)" — hires remotely worldwide; hired by Drytis for an external client. AI engineer role with strong prompt-engineering and client-facing component.

**Perfeos, Gradient Labs, AI Deploy Labs, Delphi (delphi.ai), LangWatch (langwatch.ai), Swif.ai, 14.ai (W24), Retell AI, Lightly, VOYGR, Prox, Alphawatch AI.** Common positioning theme: AI evaluation / quality operations as a bridge from trainer/QA past to AI-ops lead role, not senior ML engineer cosplay.

**LangWatch** specifically — Ilham was advised to send a 2-3 line networking email to founder Rogerio rather than a full credential dump: *"I came across LangWatch while looking at companies working on LLM evaluation. I've spent the last two years in AI evaluation and quality operations at Invisible Technologies, working on RLHF workflows, calibration, and multilingual model evaluation. A lot of the challenges LangWatch addresses are ones I've seen firsthand. I'm curious: as you've grown, what's been the most difficult aspect of evaluation for customers to get right?"* This pattern (2-3 lines of credibility + 1 thoughtful question + no resume + no job ask) is stronger than leading with a resume attachment because if they reply "tell me more about your background," they've *asked for it*.

**Braintrust** targeting — for AI evaluation companies, rank targets: A-tier = Head of Product, Product Lead, Founding PM, Head of Evaluation, Head of Customer Success/Solutions; B-tier = early PMs, Solutions Engineers, AI Evaluation Leads; C-tier = Founder/CEO (high upside, low response rate), generic recruiters. The litmus test: when John reads "I improved calibration agreement from 70% to 90% across 26 evaluators and worked on RLHF evaluation workflows," does he immediately understand why that's impressive? If yes, good target. If not, wrong target.

The deeper truth from this batch: **don't over-optimize the specific person and under-optimize volume.** A founder or PM at a 20–100 person AI-evaluation company only needs one reason to reply: "This guy has actually lived through the evaluation problems we build products for." That's already differentiated vs. 90% of inbound messages.

## Context
Came up 2026-06-17 (small companies). Ilham was applying to a batch of small AI startups and asking how to stand out to founders/PMs directly.
