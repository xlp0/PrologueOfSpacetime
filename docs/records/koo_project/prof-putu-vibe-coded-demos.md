# Prof Putu Vibe-Coded Demos

## Insight
On 2026-06-26, four demo-day presentations showed what vibe coding + domain expertise can produce in 2–3 weeks without prior software background. All guided by Prof. Ben Koo.

**1. GeoWatch for Digital Bansos** (Prof. Putu and team) — A map-based national dashboard layer that complements Kemensos' existing Cek Bansos system. Turns social-assistance records into a spatial command view: where aid is concentrated, missing, or anomalous. Not "another app" — a **control tower for social protection** that fits the Digital Bansos / Perlinsos Digital agenda under Luhut. Turns bansos from a spreadsheet problem into a situational-awareness problem.

**2. AI Voice Chat for Digital Bansos in Bahasa Daerah** — A citizen-facing access layer. Citizens who don't speak Bahasa Indonesia comfortably can voice-chat with the welfare system in their regional language: ask about eligibility, how to apply, what data is needed, why rejected, how to correct. GeoWatch = government-facing intelligence layer; this = citizen-facing access layer. "One sees the map. The other lets the villager actually get through the door."

**3. Math Game** (Prof. Yohanes Surya + Marcia + a Papuan medical undergrad) — SD–SMP math game with progression, multiple modes, addition/subtraction/multiplication/division, and a built-in database storing each child's scores. Not edutainment — both a **numeracy training tool** and a **measurement system**. Generates continuous micro-data from everyday use. The real product is a **national sensor for numeracy development**: teachers track individual progress, schools identify stagnation, government sees talent clusters and regional gaps instead of flying blind. Most countries are terrible at early talent identification because exams come too late and data arrives too slowly — this flips that.

**4. ClassOS** (Prof. Putu and team) — A "Spotify for knowledge" UI built on top of Prabowo's 300,000 smartboards. AI-generated classroom content (short explainers, stories, podcasts, songs on why bananas are curved, how rainbows form, digital privacy). Designed to fill the first hour of every school day; auto-summarizes the day's learning and emails students and parents. Turns classroom hardware into an active learning ecosystem instead of a passive screen. The smartboard purchase is easy; knowing what to do with them every day is the hard problem ClassOS solves.

## Context
Summarized 2026-06-26 (Prof Putu Research Inquiry). The vibe-coding speed was the headline: people with no prior coding experience, working with Prof. Ben Koo, demoed working software in front of Pak Luhut within 2–3 weeks.
