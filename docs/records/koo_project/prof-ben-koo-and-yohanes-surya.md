# Prof. Ben Koo & Yohanes Surya

## Insight
Two complementary figures behind the InaAI orbit. Their pairing tells you what kind of company InaAI is — not a typical AI startup, closer to a sovereign-capability initiative.

**Prof. Yohanes Surya** — Indonesia's legendary Physics Olympiad coach; founder of Surya Institute, Surya University; creator of the GASING ("Gampang, Asyik, Menyenangkan") learning method; decades of Papua education initiatives. His central question for 30 years: *"How do we create world-class Indonesian talent at scale?"* Not primarily an AI researcher or businessman — closer to nation-builder, educator, talent architect.

**Prof. Ben Koo** — MIT Engineering Systems PhD; MIT System Design & Management; University of Minnesota Industrial Engineering background; long-time Tsinghua faculty. Research sits at the intersection of systems thinking, organizational design, innovation ecosystems, knowledge management — a rare combination. If Surya asks "how do we produce talent?", Ben asks "how do organizations learn and innovate?"

A Koo + Surya influence suggests InaAI is closer to DeepMind's early philosophy, Singapore's AI strategy, Tsinghua ecosystem thinking, or sovereign AI initiatives than a typical SaaS startup. The pattern is: **build talent → build institutions → build infrastructure → build AI capability**, not "build model → raise funding → get customers." For Ilham, the intellectual overlap is striking — his essays repeatedly focus on information systems, labor markets, institutions, incentives, coordination problems, and talent visibility rather than just AI models. That's closer to Ben Koo's research areas than to a typical data scientist's worldview. The PKC conversation in 2021 didn't land because Ilham was focused on Python/data science, but five years later the alignment is much stronger.
