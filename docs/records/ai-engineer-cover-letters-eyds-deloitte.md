# AI Engineer Cover Letters — EYDS & Deloitte

## Insight
Two cover letters drafted 2026-05-19 for top-tier AI Engineer / Consultant roles. Tone calibration: *"A cover letter should feel like a firm handshake, not a TED Talk."*

**EYDS — AI Engineer (Senior) (1695874):** EYDS was pushing hard into AI transformation, Microsoft + Azure ecosystem, enterprise data systems, scalable AI delivery across ASEAN. Edge: not "I know AI" (thousands say that — weak signal), but: operated inside the machine room, improved model behavior, coordinated humans and systems, can speak both technical and operational language. Quiet confidence, no begging, no motivational fluff. Cover letter leaned on RLHF + reward modeling + multilingual NLP + AI data operations at scale + training systems/calibration/QA alignment + Python + analytics + human communication + international exposure without sounding like a corporate mannequin. Positioning: AI as infrastructure and execution — not just presentation.

**Deloitte — T&T Consultant, AI & Data (Data Scientist) (ID 111920):** Less "AI factory operations," more advisory / analytics / business problem solving / translating data into decisions. Tone: less operator, more **thinker-builder**. Still direct, still masculine, no corporate perfume. Cover letter: *"Good data work is not about dashboards and buzzwords. It is about reducing uncertainty, improving decisions, and building systems people can actually use. Too many teams chase 'AI' as theater. The valuable work is quieter than that."* Mentioned: RLHF workflows, data quality calibration, analytics reporting, recommendation systems, ML projects (computer vision to predictive modeling), comfortable moving between technical detail and broader business context. Plus IISMA Barcelona scholarship — strengthened ability to work across cultures and disciplines while representing Indonesian students internationally.

**Cross-cutting positioning principle:** the edge is not "AI enthusiasm." The edge is "operated inside the machine room of model training" — not generic AI enthusiasm. Both letters share the same DNA: direct opening, concrete observation about what AI work actually is, "I have done X, Y, Z" evidence, what draws me to *this* company specifically, close. No "passionate/thrilled/excited/dream opportunity/perfect fit." When uncertain between sounding impressive and sounding authentic, choose authentic.

## Context
Drafted 2026-05-19 (AI Engineer Cover Letter). Ilham was applying to top-tier consulting/engineering roles.
