# Job Board Stack — Tier 1 & Tier 2

## Insight
Ilham's job-board stack settled 2026-06-12. The default mistake most candidates make is optimizing the *application* (resume, cover letter) when the bottleneck is *deal flow*. The fix is to add boards where Ilham's profile fits better than corporate roles.

**Tier 1 — Daily Drivers (80% of effort):**
- **LinkedIn Jobs** (still king) — search: AI Operations, AI Trainer, AI Quality Analyst, AI Evaluator, Trust & Safety, Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Strategy Analyst, Investment Analyst, Research Analyst
- **Greenhouse Job Board** (boards.greenhouse.io) — many startups use it; often less competitive than LinkedIn Easy Apply
- **Indeed** — worth keeping, not amazing, but catches roles that never hit LinkedIn

**Tier 2 — Add Immediately:**
- **Wellfound** (formerly AngelList) — for Ilham this was huge: profile fits AI startups, data startups, analytics startups better than many corporate roles. Strong candidates get interviews here much faster than LinkedIn.
- **YC Jobs** (ycombinator.com/jobs) — massively underrated
- **eFinancialCareers** — for investment research roles

**Application volume target:** 10–20 highly targeted jobs per week with a tailored resume and cover letter — not 100. That's where the GPT/Claude workflow gives an edge. Workflow: Find Job → Paste JD → GPT Tailors Everything → 2-Minute Review → Apply.

**Role tiers:**
- *Tier 1 (Best Fit):* AI Operations, AI Quality Analyst, AI Trainer, RLHF/Model Evaluation, Trust & Safety — proven experience.
- *Tier 2 (Highest Upside):* Investment Research Analyst, Equity Research, Family Office Analyst, Market Research — actual experience now.
- *Tier 3 (Broad Market):* Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Operations Analyst — large number of openings.

For Ilham's actual 516→0 applications run earlier in 2026, the source breakdown was: LinkedIn 228, Indeed 101, Kalibrr 56, We Work Remotely 55, Greenhouse 46, Personal Company Portals 42, YC Startup Jobs 21, Jobstreet 15, Glints 15, Direct Email Outreach 15, Wellfound 7. Total ~601. The Wellfound number being so low (7) is itself diagnostic — Ilham had been over-indexing on high-volume boards where his hybrid profile doesn't differentiate, and under-indexing on the startup boards where it does.
