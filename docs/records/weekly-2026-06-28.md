## Teaching Observations

### [NEW] [Not Aware of Opportunities](file:///Users/bkoo/Documents/Development/GovTech/PKC/PrologueOfSpacetime/docs/teaching/observations/not_aware_of_opportunities.md)
* **Description**: Field observation documenting how five students consistently fail to recognize and act on trivially accessible opportunities across the Stereo System and Lego Saturn V projects.
* **Key Findings**:
  1. *Stereo System*: A complete Bose speaker/amplifier set with two subwoofers is physically present; local electrical engineers are available after returning from field trips — yet no plans or actions are conducted. Students play music from laptop speakers without thinking to use the Bose system in the same room.
  2. *Lego Saturn V*: Students were explicitly asked to buy paper and scotch tape to organize components — no one acted; the instructor had to purchase all materials.
  3. *Root Cause*: Conventional education systems suppress agency, separate planning from execution, eliminate local resourcefulness, and punish improvisation.
  4. *Remediation*: Five strategies proposed including scout roles, micro-procurement, and Representation Engine quests.
