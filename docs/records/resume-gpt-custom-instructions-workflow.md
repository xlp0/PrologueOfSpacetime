# Resume GPT Custom-Instructions Workflow

## Insight
The right move for Ilham was not Codex, not Claude Code, not a no-code platform — it was a **Custom GPT with detailed custom instructions**, loaded with: **Master Resume.pdf** (primary source of truth), **Profile.pdf** (LinkedIn, secondary source for additional context), **Resume.docx** (historical, source of alternative wording and forgotten accomplishments), **ACHIEVEMENT BANK.docx** (repository of quantified accomplishments, leadership examples, business impact — used aggressively during tailoring), and writing-style references (Motivation Letter EMAI.pdf, ETH Zurich.pdf, UNSW Motivation ISA.pdf, EY Cov Let.docx, generic cv.docx — to learn tone/voice/reasoning style/communication patterns; never copy text directly).

The workflow: input JD → GPT analyzes role / extracts ATS keywords / assesses fit / reviews files / selects strongest supporting achievements / chooses most appropriate positioning / tailors resume / generates cover letter / reviews both as a recruiter / revises before presenting. Source priority order: Master Resume → Achievement Bank → Profile → Historical Resume → Writing Samples. Positioning options: AI Operations & Quality Analytics, Data Analytics, Investment Research, Business Analytics, Strategy & Operations, Technology & Analytics, AI Training & Evaluation, Research & Analytics.

The non-obvious unlock: **upload motivation letters and cover letters as writing-style references.** Most people upload resumes; very few upload examples of their own writing. That's a big advantage — the GPT won't sound like "Applicant #274," it will have a model of your actual voice. Ilham's writing style profile: direct, analytical, reflective, grounded in evidence, systems-oriented, low-fluff, moderately intellectual, quietly ambitious. Pattern: "At first glance X appears true. But beneath the surface Y is happening. This reflects a larger systemic issue. My experience with X taught me Y. Therefore I want to work on Z." Avoid: corporate jargon, generic enthusiasm, "passionate/thrilled/excited/dream opportunity/perfect fit."

Internal recruiter review step is critical: after generating tailored resume, switch roles — act as experienced recruiter reviewing the resume for 30 seconds; evaluate ATS compatibility, recruiter appeal, technical relevance, clarity, positioning, achievement strength; identify weak bullets, missing keywords, unclear accomplishments, ATS risks; revise accordingly. **Do not show the first draft. Only show the improved final version.**

## Context
Came up 2026-06-12 (Claude vs Odyseus AI). Ilham had seen a viral LinkedIn post about a Claude Code job-application pipeline and asked whether to use Codex.
