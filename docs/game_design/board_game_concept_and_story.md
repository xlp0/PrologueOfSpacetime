# Dungeons & Dragons: Prologue of Spacetime Edition
## A Philosophical RPG Framework for the End Times

This document outlines a **customized role-playing game (RPG)** that transcends traditional board games by integrating **Dungeons & Dragons mechanics** with the **Prologue of Spacetime** philosophical framework and **The Four Dining Philosophers** cosmology.

---

## **The Story**

*The sirens wailed across Bali at midnight. Not a drill. Not a false alarm. The impossible had become inevitable.*

You are part of a small group of survivors who witnessed the first seismic readings: the entire Sunda Trench had shifted 47 meters in three minutes. The ocean is rising. Not gradually over decades, but catastrophically over **30 days**. Every climate model, every simulation, every desperate calculation points to the same horrifying conclusion: the entire island of Bali—from the beaches of Kuta to the sacred peak of Mount Agung—will be submerged beneath the waves.

You stand in the chaos of Kuta Beach as tourists flee and buildings collapse. In your hands: a laptop with climate data, a backpack of scavenged electronics, and a desperate plan. You must journey inland, moving step-by-step through Bali's real geography—from the doomed coastal zones through cultural heartlands to the highland sanctuary of Bedugul. Along the way, you'll scavenge technology, build infrastructure, learn Balinese culture, and race against time to construct a "very powerful boat" capable of surviving the deluge.

But survival isn't just about staying alive. It's about preserving civilization itself—the **Four Pillars** that define humanity's future: **Energy** to power the new world, **Communication** to connect survivors, **Time** to optimize every precious hour, and **Technology** to preserve humanity's accumulated knowledge.

Every decision matters. Every day counts. Every roll of the dice determines not just your fate, but the fate of everyone who depends on you.

**Will you become scavengers clinging to a leaky raft? Or will you become the architects of a new civilization?**

The countdown has begun. You have 30 days.

Traditional board games like Monopoly are **deterministic, zero-sum, and philosophically hollow**. This D&D variant moves toward:
- **Narrative emergence** over rigid rules
- **Collaborative survival** over competitive accumulation
- **Philosophical depth** grounded in spacetime principles
- **Educational transformation** through immersive storytelling

## **The Game Concept: The 30-Day Deluge**

### **Scenario Overview**
The world faces an **apocalyptic catastrophe** where **water levels are rising inexorably**. In **30 days**, the entire island of Bali will be submerged beneath the waves. This is not merely a physical crisis—it represents the **collapse of civilization's foundational structures**: energy grids, communication networks, technological infrastructure, knowledge repositories, and temporal continuity.

### **Primary Objective**
Players must **survive** by:
1. **Finding or creating a sanctuary** (high ground)
2. **Building a "very powerful boat"** capable of sustaining life indefinitely
3. **Preserving the Four Pillars** of civilization (Energy, Communication, Time, Technology)

### **Alternative Scenarios**
- **Zombie Apocalypse Variant:** Undead hordes replace rising waters; players must scavenge, escape, and fortify
- **Political Collapse Variant:** Societal breakdown without supernatural elements; focus on resource management and diplomacy
- **Cosmic Event Variant:** Reality itself is fragmenting; players must navigate spacetime anomalies

## **Key Innovations: Bridging D&D and Philosophy**

### **1. AI Dungeon Master (The Computational)**
Instead of a human Dungeon Master, the game employs a **Large Language Model (LLM)** as the narrative engine:
- **Dynamic Story Generation:** Creates branching narratives based on player choices
- **Contextual Narration:** Reads scenarios aloud with dramatic flair
- **Adaptive Difficulty:** Adjusts challenges based on party composition and progress
- **Philosophical Integration:** Weaves spacetime principles into every encounter
- **Multi-modal Interaction:** Combines text, voice, and visual elements (optional)

**Technical Implementation:** The AI DM operates as a **Single Source of Truth (SSOT) Protocol**, maintaining narrative consistency while allowing emergent gameplay.

### **2. Infrastructure & Equipment as Game Mechanics (The Power Systems)**

Replacing traditional D&D ability scores—which typically measure a single hero's innate physical or mental traits (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, etc.)—characters in this scenario are defined by their **access to systems, networks, and infrastructure**. 

The Four Pillars represent the **attributes of the party's technological ecosystem**. You don't "level up" your biological body; you level up your grid, your bandwidth, and your processing power.

#### **Energy (ENE)** - *The Power Grid*
- **Concept:** Electrical generation, battery storage, and fuel reserves.
- **Game Mechanic:** Determines what equipment can be brought online. It's not about human stamina; it's about having the juice to run the infrastructure.
- **Example:** ENE 8 means you rely on a few scavenged car batteries. ENE 18 means students have successfully wired a stable microgrid or continuous hydroelectric generator.

#### **Communication (COM)** - *The Network*
- **Concept:** Bandwidth, network topology, routing protocols, and student connectivity.
- **Game Mechanic:** Represents your ability to transmit data and connect disparate systems or survivor groups.
- **Example:** COM 10 is a localized line-of-sight radio frequency. COM 18 represents restoring a robust local mesh network allowing coordinated, real-time action across vast distances.

#### **Time (TIM)** - *The Optimization Engine*
- **Concept:** Automation, algorithmic efficiency, and background processing.
- **Game Mechanic:** Replaces "Initiative/Dexterity". High TIM means you have systems (scripts, IoT sensors, automated routines) doing the heavy lifting passively, buying the party more actual hours in the day.
- **Example:** TIM 17 means automated drones or scripts handle daily monitoring and supply inventory, freeing the human operators to focus entirely on high-level survival strategy.

#### **Technology / Computation (TEC)** - *Processing Power*
- **Concept:** Hardware capability, AI execution, raw computational throughput, and data storage/archives.
- **Game Mechanic:** Your capacity to run complex simulations, crunch variables, host LLMs, and preserve critical information.
- **Example:** If you only have access to a random **Raspberry Pi** or isolated office PC, your computational attribute is weak (TEC 8). If you manage to establish a network connection to an **NVIDIA B200 supercomputer** with comprehensive data archives, your party's TEC instantly spikes to 20+, fundamentally altering your ability to solve complex problems and access preserved knowledge.

### **3. Alignment with Spacetime Principles**

The game mechanics embody **Homotopy Type Theory (HoTT)** concepts:
- **Types as Spaces:** Each character class represents a distinct "space" in the game's possibility landscape
- **Equality as Paths:** Character progression follows "paths" through skill trees and narrative choices
- **Propositions as Types:** Game rules are encoded as type-theoretic propositions
- **Universal Properties:** Core mechanics (like the four Stats) serve as universal building blocks

**Cubical Logic Model (CLM) Integration:**
- **Abstract Specification:** Game rules and win conditions
- **Concrete Implementation:** Actual gameplay sessions and player actions
- **Balanced Experience:** The AI DM mediates between rules and narrative flow

## **Gameplay Mechanics: The d20 Protocol**

### **Core Resolution System**
Like traditional D&D, the game uses **d20 rolls** to determine uncertain outcomes, but the modifiers apply to the *systems* you are leveraging rather than your physical muscles:

1. **Roll 1d20** (twenty-sided die)
2. **Add relevant system attribute modifier** (ENE, COM, KNO, TIM, or TEC)
3. **Add user proficiency bonus** (if the character is trained in operating that specific system)
4. **Compare to Difficulty Class (DC)** set by the AI DM
5. **Success if Total ≥ DC**

### **Attribute Modifiers**
System attributes (1-20) convert to modifiers using the standard formula. The higher the score, the more robust that aspect of your power system is:
```
Modifier = (System Score - 10) ÷ 2 (rounded down)

Examples:
Connected to B200 (TEC 20) → +5 modifier
Local Mesh Network (COM 14) → +2 modifier
Scavenged Batteries (ENE 8) → -1 modifier
```

### **Unique Innovation: Auto-Success Threshold**
**If your infrastructure's base attribute already exceeds the DC, the party automatically succeeds without rolling.**

This creates a highly satisfying **progression curve** where acquiring better tech fundamentally changes the game:
- **Early game:** You have low-tier equipment (Raspberry Pis, walkie-talkies). You must roll the d20 for almost everything.
- **Mid game:** As you build servers and power banks, basic logistical challenges auto-succeed. You only roll for complex emergencies.
- **Late game:** With supercomputers and mesh networks online, you auto-succeed at almost all standard tasks, freeing you to focus on the massive existential threats of the rising waters.

**Example:**
- Running an advanced fluid-dynamics simulation requires TEC DC 16.
- A party relying on a standard laptop (TEC 10, +0 modifier) must roll d20 and get 16+ to succeed.
- A party hooked into the B200 Supercomputer (TEC 20, +5 modifier) mathematically exceeds the DC 16 with their base score alone—the simulation completes instantly and flawlessly.

### **World Map: The Island of Bali (Realistic Geography)**

The game takes place on the **real island of Bali, Indonesia**, using authentic geography, elevations, and cultural context. Players cannot simply "invent" survival in a vacuum—they must navigate Bali's actual terrain, moving step-by-step through connected locations as rising waters force them toward the highlands.

#### **Bali Geography Overview**
- **Total Area:** 140 km × 80 km volcanic island
- **Highest Point:** Mount Agung (3,031m) - Bali's sacred volcano
- **Highland Sanctuary:** Bedugul region (1,200-1,500m elevation)
- **Coastal Risk Zones:** Kuta, Sanur, Tanjung Benoa (0-10m elevation)
- **Climate:** Tropical with distinct highland/coastal differences

#### **The Tsunami/Flood Scenario (Realistic Threat)**
Based on real tsunami risk assessments:
- **High-Risk Coastal Areas:** Kuta, Seminyak, Sanur, Tanjung Benoa, Nusa Dua, Canggu (will flood first)
- **Safe Highland Zones:** Bedugul, Kintamani, Mount Batur region, Ubud highlands
- **Tsunami Range:** Can reach 1+ mile inland; low-lying areas completely submerged
- **Evacuation Time:** 15-20 minutes warning from sirens to reach high ground

**Game Premise:** A catastrophic mega-tsunami is approaching Bali. Players start in coastal areas and must journey to **Bedugul** (the highland sanctuary) while gathering resources, building infrastructure, and ultimately constructing a seaworthy vessel capable of surviving the 30-day deluge.

### **Resource Locations & The Four Pillars (Decentralization Mechanic)**

Because disasters can strike specific locations, **players must decentralize their infrastructure**. If you build all your servers in Ubud and a landslide destroys it, your entire TEC attribute is wiped out.

**Strategic Distribution:**
- **Energy (ENE) Hubs:** Kintamani (geothermal), Bedugul (hydro), Tabanan (agricultural)

- **Time (TIM) Hubs:** Bedugul (coordination), Kintamani (early warning), Ubud (logistics)

1. **Identify Need:** The sanctuary is low on TEC processing power. If the party loses connection to an offline server, their processing power drops.
2. **Expedition Planning:** The party allocates TIME to travel to a specific node (e.g., the Silicon Hub).
3. **Resolution:** The AI DM runs the encounter (hazard navigation, hardware extraction).
4. **Extraction & Progression:** The party returns with specific physical components (e.g., 2GB RAM modules, SSDs, soldering irons, 3D printers) to build or repair localized infrastructure.

### **Event Deck & Random Disasters (The Timeline)**
The 30 days are not a rigid, predictable script. Instead of knowing exactly when a blackout or storm will hit, the AI DM uses a randomized **Event Deck** (similar to drawing Chance or Community Chest cards in Monopoly).

**Drawing Events:** 
Every 2-3 days (or when players trigger certain conditions), an Event Card is drawn. Examples include:
- *"Jailbreak/Asteroid Event":* A localized disaster strikes one of the map nodes. If the players centralized all their servers at that node, they lose those attributes entirely until they rebuild.
- *"RAM Price Spike":* The IDR cost to craft or trade for server memory doubles for 2 days.
- *"Global Blackout":* A massive electromagnetic storm drops everyone's COM modifier to 0 for 1 day.

Players must foresee these possibilities and prepare (e.g., building redundant backup servers at multiple different locations so a localized earthquake doesn't end their game).

### **Balinese Cultural Mechanics: Language Discount System**

In authentic Bali culture, **speaking Balinese language** creates trust and reduces prices. This mechanic rewards cultural engagement.

#### **Language Proficiency Levels**

Players can learn Balinese phrases through:
- **Interacting with locals** (COM checks)
- **Studying at Ubud cultural centers** (KNO checks + time investment)
- **Hiring Balinese language tutor NPC** (costs 2,000,000 IDR, grants +2 COM with locals)

**Proficiency Tiers:**
1. **None (International Language Only):** Standard prices, -2 penalty on COM checks with locals
2. **Basic (5 phrases):** 10% discount on all purchases from Balinese NPCs
3. **Conversational (20 phrases):** 20% discount + priority access to resources
4. **Fluent (50+ phrases):** 30% discount + unlock hidden quests and cultural knowledge

#### **Essential Balinese Phrases for Discounts**

**Greetings & Respect:**
- **"Om Swastiastu"** (ohm swas-tee-as-too) - Sacred greeting (most respectful)
- **"Suksma"** (sook-sma) - Thank you
- **"Ampura"** (am-poo-ra) - Sorry/Excuse me

**Shopping & Bargaining:**
- **"Aji kuda?"** (ah-jee koo-da) - How much?
- **"Mahal pisan"** (ma-hal pee-san) - Too expensive
- **"Tawar"** (ta-war) - Bargain/negotiate
- **"Dados"** (da-dos) - Agreed/OK

**Survival Phrases:**
- **"Icang lakar ka Bedugul"** (ee-chang la-kar ka be-doo-gul) - I want to go to Bedugul
- **"Apa ada bahaya?"** (a-pa a-da ba-ha-ya) - Is there danger?
- **"Tolong tiang"** (to-long tee-ang) - Help me

**Cultural Respect:**
- **"Matur suksma"** (ma-tur sook-sma) - Thank you very much (formal)
- **"Ratu"** (ra-too) - Respectful address (like "sir/madam")

## **The 30-Day Timeline: God of Time in Action**

The **God of Time** manifests as the game's ultimate constraint, divided into distinct phases punctuated by **world-altering events**. A mere objective of survival is not enough to drive a compelling story; players must navigate a dynamic, escalating series of crises affecting the Four Pillars.

### **Phase 1: Evacuation and Resource Gathering (Day 1-10)**
- **Day 3 – The First Surge:** An unexpected acceleration in the water rise wipes out Kuta and Seminyak. A sudden test of the party's mobility and **Energy (ENE)**.
- **Day 7 – The Global Blackout:** The last organized satellite and internet infrastructures collapse. Radio silence falls over Bali, testing the party's **Communication (COM)** networks.

### **Phase 2: Highland Migration and Consolidation (Day 11-20)**
- **Day 12 – The Data Center Siege:** A powerful rival faction attempts to hoard or destroy crucial data centers in Denpasar. The party must intervene to protect the pillar of **Technology (TEC)**.
- **Day 18 – The Grid Death:** Regional power plants finally succumb to the waters. The party must have secured independent power generation via **Technology (TEC)** and **Energy (ENE)**, or face total darkness.

### **Phase 3: Crisis Escalation (Day 21-27)**
- **Day 22 – The Faction Wars:** As dry land becomes exponentially scarce, desperate survivor groups violently clash for Bedugul and Kintamani. The party must use **Communication (COM)** and tactical strategy to navigate or avoid the conflict.
- **Day 26 – Resource Attrition:** Fresh water and uncompromised food supplies become critically low due to salinization and contamination, straining the team's survival skills.

### **Phase 4: The Final Countdown (Day 28-30)**
- **Day 29 – The Sanctuary Test:** A catastrophic storm or structural failure threatens the party's nearly complete sanctuary/boat. A frantic, all-hands-on-deck test of every acquired skill and stat.
- **Day 30 – The Zenith Tide:** The final, island-swallowing wave. Only those with a completed sanctuary and mastery of the Four Pillars survive the transition into the new flooded epoch.

### **Endgame Success Conditions (Measuring the Post-Deluge Epoch)**
In traditional D&D, success is defined by killing the final "boss monster." In this framework, **the true goal is not just surviving Day 30, but measuring *how* the party survived and the quality of the future they secured.** 

At the end of the game, the AI DM tallies the party's final status across their Four Pillars—their power grid, food reserves, computation layer, and accumulated knowledge—to generate their final "Epoch Rating."

- **Tier 1: Scavenger Survival (Barely Alive):** The party is alive on a leaky raft with a few weeks of food, scavenged batteries, and no data. They survived the Deluge, but face a bleak, uncertain future.
- **Tier 2: The Sustainable Outpost (Settler):** The party has a stable floating sanctuary, renewable micro-hydro power, and a functioning radio matrix. They can survive indefinitely (for the next 10-20 years) but cannot meaningfully advance or rebuild civilization.
- **Tier 3: The New Light (Architect):** The sanctuary is a technological marvel. It is powered by supercomputers with comprehensive archives (TEC 20+), automated by advanced drone logistics (TIM 18+), sustainably powered (ENE 18+), and connected via robust networks (COM 16+). They are not merely survivors; they are the founding fathers of the next civilization.

Alternatively, elite players log their runs for **"Speedrunning" or "Efficiency"**—attempting to achieve Architect Tier sustainability using the minimal amount of resources or in the fewest possible days, creating a highly replayable challenge.

### **Time Management Mechanics:**
- Each action consumes **time units** (hours or days)
- Players must balance **urgent vs. important** tasks with extreme time pressure
- The AI DM tracks time automatically and narrates temporal pressure
- High TIM stat allows **time optimization** (completing tasks 25-50% faster)
- **Critical:** With only 30 days, every decision matters—no time for mistakes!
