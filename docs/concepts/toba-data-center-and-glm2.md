# Toba Data Center & GLM2 Stack

## Insight
The InaAI orbit runs on a sovereign infrastructure stack: a data center at Toba hosting **GLM2** (an open-source model that team claims is "better than ChatGPT and Claude" — almost certainly the wrong framing). The realistic interpretation is not "Indonesia built a foundation model that crushed OpenAI." It is: Indonesia is assembling a sovereign AI system from open-source models + local infrastructure + local workflows + local data + maybe fine-tuning / RAG / wrappers. The win for government is controllable, private, local, customizable, good-enough, cheaper than paying foreign APIs forever, and aligned to Indonesian use cases — not best model on Earth.

The pkc/GitHub/Kubernetes/opencode/antigravity pipeline around GLM2 is the actual engineering reality. Toba is also politically significant: linked to Luhut Binsar Pandjaitan's business group (TBS Energi Utama, IDX: TOBA), a coal company pivoting to energy transition + EV ecosystem (Electrum JV with GoTo). The data center at Toba ties InaAI into a broader political-commercial story: coal cash flow + political access + EV/AI narrative. ZeroTier virtual hosting is used for secure internal access.

The "GLM2 better than ChatGPT" claim is almost certainly overselling. Possible realities: (1) using an open-source model family and someone in the room is hyping it; (2) using a local stack that feels good on narrow demos (Indonesian context, curated internal knowledge, retrieval over local documents, scripted flows, good prompting/tool use) — the *system* looks good even if the base model is not frontier-level; (3) they don't actually mean "better overall" — they mean cheaper, local, private, deployable in-house, sufficient for gov use cases, better on a narrow benchmark or Indonesian task.
