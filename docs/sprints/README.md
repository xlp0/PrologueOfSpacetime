## 1. Directory Structure & Lifecycle Allocation

To avoid cognitive overload and context fragmentation, sprints transition through a deterministic lifecycle:
* **Active Work Area (`_active/`)**: The staging ground where in-flight sprints are refined, executed, and tested against their Definitions of Done.
* **Allocated Completed Categories (`01-` through `07-` and `epoch-01-` through `epoch-04-`)**: Once a sprint satisfies its DoD, its verified snapshot is allocated to its designated functional domain directory.
