# Second Brain as a Sellable Agency Product

## Insight
The same markdown second brain that's a personal tool (see [[second-brain-obsidian-foundations]]) is also a packaged service you can sell to non-AI-savvy businesses. The pitch rests on a concrete pain point: every business is "drowning" in 15 disconnected tools — nothing talks to anything, everyone's still copy-pasting into ChatGPT. A single brain of clients, reviews, jobs, and invoices that the whole team can query is the fix, and most businesses can't build it themselves.

The economics the author quotes: a setup like this goes for **$2,000–$3,000 with a monthly retainer** on top. The deliverable is, technically, "a combination of folders and files" plus a visual layer (see [[visual-graph-layer-second-brain]]) — the value is in assembling and wiring it to that business's specific world, not in any proprietary software.

Two tiers structure the offer:

- **Free version** — the graph tool with the author's branding (AI Workshop logo). Functional, self-hosted, runs on localhost. Good enough to demo and to use yourself.
- **White-label paid version** — rebrandable, plus 2D and 3D variants and a v3 roadmap, sold through a paid community that also covers how to *sell* the setup to businesses and a beginner Claude Code course.

The reusable business model: the second brain is open (markdown, files, a GitHub-installable viewer), so the moat isn't the code — it's the **integration work** per client: mapping their tools and entities into the vault, wiring [[second-brain-business-os-wiring]]-style MCP connections, and training the team to query it. This is the services layer on top of the [[agentic-os-shared-brain]] pattern — the brain is shared infrastructure, the agency sells the install, the tuning, and the ongoing retainer.
