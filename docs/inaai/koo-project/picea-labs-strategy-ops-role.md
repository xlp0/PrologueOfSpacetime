# Picea Labs — Strategy & Operations Advisor

## Insight
**Picea Labs** is a small AI-native company that helps small businesses make smarter pricing decisions by combining software and consulting. The **Strategy & Operations Advisor** role (Wellfound, 2026-06-19) was the strongest fit of the Wellfound batch.

The role: own the recurring client relationship, make data trustworthy and legible, advise owners on pricing and adjacent business questions, execute the process in existing tooling. Shadow the founder's client calls for 1–2 months, then transition to primary client contact. Identify patterns from real client conversations and translate them into product improvement ideas. Develop and write the client-service playbook. Eventually hire and lead the client service team. *"Help small businesses make big decisions as a trusted advisor and figure out patterns across clients."*

Comp: $36k–$60k + 0.0%–1.0% equity; remote; contract; 9 AM–3 PM ET collaboration hours. Hiring contact: Nathaniel Schwalb (Founder).

**Why this was the strongest fit:** values analytical thinking + client advising + ambiguity more than deep technical specialization. Picea's "What This Role is NOT" section explicitly says: not a sales role; not solely operational manager; not just customer success; not a corporate role. That fits Ilham's hybrid profile (AI operations + analytics + advising instincts) better than a pure ML engineering role. Positioning leaned on **RLHF + calibration 70%→90% across 26 QAs** as transferable to "helped small businesses make smarter pricing decisions by combining software and consulting" — both are about turning messy reality into legible, trustworthy decisions.

The role is a strong demonstration of the broader positioning principle: for early-stage startups that combine software + consulting, an AI-operations/quality-analytics background is often a better fit than a pure SWE or pure consultant background — because the work is operational, multi-disciplinary, and judgment-driven.

## Context
Applied 2026-06-19 (Standing Out as a Candidate). Was one of 5 companies in the batch: Awaz.ai, Ilant Health, Lens, Jobrooms, Picea Labs.
