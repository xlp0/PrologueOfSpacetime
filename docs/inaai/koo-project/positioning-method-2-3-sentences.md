# Positioning Method — 2–3 Sentences Max

## Insight
Every "stand out" answer on Wellfound was compressed to 2–3 sentences after recruiter-skim feedback ("too long" was the recurring note). The strongest pattern across all of Ilham's positioning: **lead with the RLHF + calibration metric, not generic AI enthusiasm.**

Canonical 2-3 sentence template: *"I've spent the past two years evaluating and improving LLM performance through RLHF, model evaluation, and quality analytics. I improved calibration alignment from 70% to 90% across 26 Quality Analysts and have evaluated thousands of model outputs across multilingual reasoning tasks. I'm particularly interested in [domain the company works in — information quality, agent reliability, etc.]."*

Even shorter variant: *"I bring hands-on experience in RLHF, model evaluation, and quality analytics, having evaluated thousands of LLM outputs and improved calibration alignment from 70% to 90% across 26 Quality Analysts. I'm especially interested in building AI systems that are reliable, transparent, and trustworthy."*

The strongest signal in Ilham's profile is **not** "AI." It is: *"I've spent two years inside the messy reality of measuring AI quality."* Very few people can genuinely say that. Most applicants have built projects; Ilham has actually run evaluation workflows, coordinated reviewers, improved calibration, and worked on multilingual model assessment. That is the angle to lead with every time.

Anti-pattern call-out from the same convo: don't oversell into research-engineer fantasy. For ML Engineer roles, Ilham's fit was 65–70/100 — strong on evaluation/RLHF/prompting/human-in-the-loop, but no evidence of production ML engineering, fine-tuning models, building inference pipelines, LangGraph/CrewAI/AutoGen, or Hugging Face/PyTorch in production. The "stand out" answer should sell evaluation/RLHF/calibration/information quality strengths rather than trying to sound like a senior ML engineer. Positioning will come across as much more believable to the founder.

**Disclosure question**: don't tell recruiters you used AI — they care about outcomes, declaring AI can trigger skepticism.

## Context
Came up 2026-06-19 (Standing Out as a Candidate) for Awaz.ai, Ilant Health, Lens (ICR + ML Engineer), Jobrooms, Picea Labs. Each "stand out" answer compressed to 2-3 sentences after recruiter-skim feedback.
