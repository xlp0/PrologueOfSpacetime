# InaAI — Indonesian Sovereign AI

## Insight
InaAI (inaai.ai) is an Indonesian AI company positioned around **Sovereign AI** — locally governed, secure AI for enterprises, government, and strategic industries rather than relying entirely on US/China frontier models. The mission is less "build another ChatGPT" and more "help governments, banks, telcos, and enterprises deploy AI safely." Public information is surprisingly sparse — founders, exec team, investors, and funding rounds are not widely available. The website language is nation-building oriented rather than startup-growth-hacking oriented. Likely early-stage ambiguity: unclear processes, evolving priorities, multiple hats.

The strategic positioning sits at the intersection of AI + policy + infrastructure, which is unusual and potentially interesting. Sovereign AI matters because: data stays under local control; models can be adapted to Indonesian language, culture, regulations; institutions retain control over critical AI infrastructure; Indonesia develops AI capability rather than only consuming foreign products. Sits inside a broader national trend that includes Indonesia's AI roadmap, sovereign AI fund discussions, domestic compute infrastructure, and AI talent development programs.

For Ilham's fit: the profile aligns unusually well. Most candidates are either ML engineers (no institutions/policy sense) or policy people (cannot build/evaluate AI). Ilham sits in the middle — RLHF, model evaluation, multilingual evaluation, calibration, AI operations, plus Indonesian development perspective. Strong-fit roles: AI Operations, AI Quality, Evaluation & Alignment, AI Governance Operations, Trust & Safety, AI Localization, Knowledge Systems. Weaker fit: Frontier ML Research, Core Model Training.
