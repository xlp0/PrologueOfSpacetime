# How to Build a 3D Printed Solar RC Toy Car

This document explains how to construct a solar-powered toy car using 3D printed parts and hardware components.

## 3D Printed Components and Dimensions

The following parts must be generated using a 3D printer.

- **Chassis** (1 piece)
  - Length: 145 mm
  - Width: 65 mm
  - Plate thickness: 4 mm
  - Axle hole diameter: 4 mm

- **Driven Gear** (1 piece)
  - Tip diameter: 25 mm
  - Number of teeth: 23
  - Face width: 5 mm
  - Center bore diameter: 2.4 mm

- **Pinion Gear** (1 piece)
  - Tip diameter: 12 mm
  - Number of teeth: 10
  - Face width: 5 mm
  - Center bore diameter: 2.4 mm

- **Wheels** (4 pieces)
  - Outer diameter: 35 mm
  - Tread width: 12 mm
  - Center bore diameter: 2.4 mm

## Required Hardware Components

Gather these hardware parts for the final assembly.

- 2 Axles
- 1 Dynamo (DC Motor)
- 2 Solar Panels

## Assembly Instructions

1. Print the chassis, driven gear, pinion gear, and four wheels using a 3D printer.
2. Insert both axles into the four axle holder holes located under the chassis.
3. Attach the driven gear onto the rear axle.
4. Mount the four wheels onto the ends of the two axles.
5. Secure the dynamo to the chassis structure.
6. Attach the pinion gear onto the dynamo shaft, ensuring it directly meshes with the driven gear.
7. Mount the two solar panels on top of the chassis and wire them to the dynamo.
