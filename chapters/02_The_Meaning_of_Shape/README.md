### 4. Foundational Connections

#### HoTT and Spatial Types
In **Homotopy Type Theory**, we treat **Types as Spaces** and **Terms as Points**. A Shape is a Type; its boundary is the collection of Paths between points. The Kinect reads space into Types; the Printer writes Types into space. This round-trip is the operational realization of the **Propositions-as-Types** correspondence applied to Geometry.

#### Agentic Role: The Coder
In the **Miner-Coder-Trader Triad**, shaping is the Coder's act of **Value Seeing**—transforming abstract specifications (CAD models, depth maps) into concrete implementations (physical objects, spatial data). The PCard executes the transformation.

#### Universality: Coproducts and Products
Geometry operationalizes the **Universal Properties** of Coproduct ($A + B$, choice/boundary) and Product ($A \times B$, combination/intersection). Every spatial relation is built from these two universal constructions—the same constructions that underlie all data types in computation.
